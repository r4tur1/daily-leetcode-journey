## Why this repository exists

This is my daily practice space for LeetCode. Each entry captures not just the final solution, but the thinking behind it: the pattern I recognized, the trade-offs I considered, and the lesson I want to remember.

The goal is simple: **show up, solve deliberately, and improve continuously.**

## Solution format

Every solution aims to include:

1. **Problem** — a short restatement and link to LeetCode.
2. **Approach** — the key observation and chosen pattern.
3. **Proof / reasoning** — why the approach works.
4. **Complexity** — time and space complexity.
5. **Implementation** — clean, readable code.
6. **Reflection** — what I learned and what I would revisit.

## Rules for the journey

- Practice every day, even if the session is short.
- Prefer understanding over collecting solved counts.
- Write down mistakes while they are still fresh.
- Revisit difficult problems instead of hiding them.
- Keep solutions focused, tested, and easy to explain.
